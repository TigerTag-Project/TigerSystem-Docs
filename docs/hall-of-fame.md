## Join the fight

There is no application, no form and no approval. Do any one of these and your
name lands on this page the next time the list is refreshed:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Open an issue** | Something wrong, unclear, or missing — in the docs, an app, the protocol. A precise report is worth more than a polite one. |
| **Send a pull request** | One subject at a time, on the page that owns it. [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) has the rules; they are short. |
| **Translate** | English is the source, French is derived. A new language starts with a single page. |
| **Build something compatible** | A reader, an app, a printer integration, a shell to print. Say *"compatible with TigerTag"* freely — no permission, no fee. |
| **Break it and tell us** | Chip, reader, app version, what you did, what it showed. That is the report that gets fixed. |

Other ways to help, including the ones that cost money and the ones that cost
nothing, are on [Support the project](./support.md).
