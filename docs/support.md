# Support the project

The applications are free. The protocol is open. The TigerPOD shell is a free
download, the TigerScale is open hardware anyone may build and sell, and the
chips work whether you buy ours or the cheapest NTAG on a marketplace.

None of that pays for itself. Here is what actually does — and, since the whole
point of an open standard is that you owe it nothing, what you can safely
ignore. If you have not started yet, start free:
[start for $0](./guides/start-for-free.md).

## Buy the official parts, if you were buying anyway

The protocol is deliberately **not tied to official chips**: any blank NTAG
bought anywhere works identically, and nothing blocks it. But when you do need
chips or a reader, buying the official ones funds the standard rather than a
marketplace listing.

- [TigerTag chips](./products/tigertag.md) — stickers and
 refill carriers, from the official shop or a reseller.
- The [TigerPOD](./products/tigerpod.md) electronics kit — the same two readers
 you would source yourself, with the project's logo on them, usually cheaper
 than buying the parts separately.

Building the whole thing from generic parts is **not a lesser path**. It is the
same Pod, the same protocol, the same result.

## Wear it

The **official cap** and the **short-sleeve polo** live at
[tigersystem.io/goodies](https://tigersystem.io/goodies). Sizes, prices and
availability are on that page — it owns them, this one does not.

It is the least useful way to help and the most visible one, which is rather
the point: a cap in a makerspace explains the project faster than a link.

## Contribute what you know

Nothing here costs money, and some of it is worth more than money.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fix the documentation** | Every page has an edit link. A wrong sentence you correct is one nobody else trips on — see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). |
| **Translate** | English is the source, French is derived. A third language starts with one page. |
| **Build something compatible** | A reader, an app, a printer integration. Say *"compatible with TigerTag"* freely — no permission, no fee ([trademark policy](../TRADEMARK.md)). |
| **Get your name on the [Hall of Fame](./hall-of-fame.md)** | Every issue and every pull request lands there. No application, no approval. |
| **Report what breaks** | A precise bug report — chip, reader, app version, what you did, what it showed — is a contribution. |
| **Tag your spools, and say so** | Adoption is the argument. Every tagged spool and every manufacturer who asks their supplier about it moves the standard further than we can. |

## Ask your filament brand

The single most useful thing anyone has ever done for this project is ask a
filament manufacturer why their spools are not tagged. Six brands ship TigerTag
from the factory today ([the registry](./certified-partners.md)); each one
started with customers asking.
