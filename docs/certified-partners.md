# Certified partners

This is the registry [`TRADEMARK.md`](../TRADEMARK.md) points to: **the
authoritative list of who is allowed to put the TigerTag mark on a product.**

If you are holding a spool or a box carrying the TigerTag logo, the maker
should be on this page. If it isn't, the mark is not authorized — the logo may
be shown freely in an app, in documentation or in a store listing to say *"this
works with TigerTag"*, but never **on** a chip, a carrier, a spool or its
packaging.

## What certification actually is

An **audit**, granted by TigerSystem — of the product and of the process — and
then a trademark licence. It is not paperwork and it is not automatic: it is
what makes the promise on the box true.

For a filament manufacturer it means one thing in particular: they are given
the **signing tools**. Their spools can carry a
[**TigerTag+ Certified**](./products/tigertag-plus.md) — a catalogue identity
*plus* an origin signature, issued under a private key TigerTag holds. Anyone
can verify that signature, **offline, on their own phone, with no account**. A
cloned tag fails the check.

Certification is never required to *use* the protocol. Implementing it,
building on it, and saying *"compatible with TigerTag"* stay free, unaudited
and self-declared — see
[third-party hardware](./compatibility/third-party-hardware.md) for what that
looks like in practice.

## Integration in progress

**[Nanovia](https://nanovia.tech)** and **[Filforme](https://www.filforme.com)**
are currently integrating TigerTag+. They are **not yet shipping**, and are
listed here because they are in the programme — not to imply availability.

## Checking a mark yourself

Two independent checks, and neither needs us:

- **This page.** The maker of a chip, carrier, spool or box bearing the mark
 should appear above.
- **The signature.** A TigerTag+ Certified carries an origin signature you can
 verify with any reader, offline and without an account — the public keys are
 published. See [TigerTag+](./products/tigertag-plus.md).

## Becoming certified

If you make filament, resin, inlays, carriers, a device or an application and
want to be listed here, the process is an audit followed by a trademark
licence. It is a paid service, on the model the Connectivity Standards
Alliance uses for Zigbee and Matter.
