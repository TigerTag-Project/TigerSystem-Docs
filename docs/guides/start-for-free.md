# Run the whole thing for nothing

You do not have to buy anything. Not a chip, not a reader, not a scale, not a
Pod. A phone you already own plus free software is the complete ecosystem —
inventory, identification, printer telemetry, the lot.

This page exists because almost nobody believes that on first contact, and the
reason is worth naming.

## Why everyone overestimates the cost

The parts of TigerSystem you can *see* are the physical ones: a chip, a
TigerPOD, a TigerScale, a spool. They photograph well, they sit on a desk,
they have a price. So they become "the system" in people's heads, and the
question that follows is always *how much does it cost?*

But those objects are **demonstrators** — a
[sandbox, on purpose](../vision/why-tigersystem.md), built to show what the
protocol makes possible. The system itself is the protocol, the identity, the
inventory and the apps: invisible, weightless, and free. What you can see is
the smallest part of it, and the only part with a price tag.

## What you already own replaces what looks mandatory

| What looks mandatory | What you already own | What buying it actually buys |
|---|---|---|
| An NFC chip | nothing at all — a **TigerData** spool | the spool identifies itself when you set it down |
| An NFC reader | your phone | reading without picking the phone up |
| A TigerScale | your kitchen scale | the weight without typing it |
| A TigerPOD | — | writing a spool's two chips in one pass |

The right-hand column is the whole point: every purchase **automates a
gesture**. None of them unlocks a capability. There is no feature behind a
paywall, because there is no paywall.

## No chip at all: TigerData

The protocol has a fully digital state. A **TigerData** spool carries the same
identity as a chipped one — brand, material, colour, diameter, temperatures,
grams remaining — with no chip, no UID, nothing stuck to anything. Your whole
inventory can live there.

A digital spool is [promotable to a real chip](../concepts/universal-filament-identity.md)
at any time, and nothing is lost in the move. The chip is the comfort, never
the prerequisite.

## The reader is your phone

Every recent smartphone has an NFC reader in it. **[Tiger NFC Connect](../products/tigertag-connect.md)**
is free on iOS and Android, and **[Tiger Studio](../products/tiger-studio.md)**
is free and open source (MIT) on Windows, macOS and Linux. Between them you
read chips, write chips, and manage the inventory — without a single dedicated
reader on the desk.

## The weight, by hand

Type the grams in. That is all a scale does for you. Weigh the spool on the
kitchen scale you already have, enter the number, and the inventory updates
exactly as it would otherwise.

The **[TigerScale](../products/tigerscale.md)** removes the typing — set the
spool down, it weighs, identifies and syncs on its own. It automates the
gesture; it never made the gesture possible.

## Your printers, without buying anything

Connecting a printer costs nothing either. Tiger Studio and the mobile app
talk to printers over your own network or through the manufacturer's cloud,
and what each path allows — telemetry, camera, job progress, and what it takes
to send orders back to the machine — is documented per brand in
[compatibility](../compatibility/README.md).

## If one day you want more

Then everything is still open. The [TigerPOD](../products/tigerpod.md) is a
free STL you print yourself; the [TigerScale](../products/tigerscale.md) is
MIT — printed shell, browser-flashed firmware, common parts from any supplier.
Build them, modify them, sell them. Nothing is bought from us by obligation.

And if you would rather help anyway, [supporting the project](../support.md)
lists the ways — including the ones that cost nothing.
